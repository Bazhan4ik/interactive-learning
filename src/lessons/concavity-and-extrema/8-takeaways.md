- **Comparison:** $f' \le g'$ gives $f \le g$ only with a **common anchor** $f(a) = g(a)$, and the inequality reverses to the anchor's left. Strict derivative inequalities give strict bounds, like $x - \frac{x^2}{2} < \ln(1+x) < x$.
- **Concavity:** $f''$ controls how **slopes** change, independently of whether the values rise or fall.
- **Inflection points:** check the domain, continuity, and an actual **change** of concavity. A zero of $f''$ alone, or a change across a missing input, is not enough.
- **First derivative test:** at a continuous interior point, $+ \to -$ gives a local maximum and $- \to +$ gives a local minimum, even if $f'(c)$ doesn't exist. Critical points are candidates, not conclusions.
- **Physics:** while an ideal RC circuit charges, $Q$ increases and is concave down, and the current $I = Q'$ decays to $0$.
